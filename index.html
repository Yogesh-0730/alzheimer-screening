Clinical Warning Indicators

Early Signs & Symptoms

Recognizing cognitive changes at an early stage allows timely lifestyle, clinical, and pharmacological interventions.

🧠

Short-Term Memory Loss

Forgetting recently learned information, asking the same questions repeatedly, and relying heavily on memory aids.

🧩

Difficulty Problem Solving

Challenges in developing and following a plan, working with numbers, balancing checkbooks, or tracking monthly bills.

⏳

Confusion with Time & Place

Losing track of dates, seasons, the passage of time, or forgetting where they are and how they arrived there.

👁️

Visual & Spatial Disruption

Difficulty judging distance, determining color or contrast, and navigating familiar physical environments.

🗣️

New Word Finding Problems

Trouble following or joining a conversation, stopping in the middle of a sentence, or calling items by incorrect names.

🛡️

Changes in Mood & Judgment

Decreased judgment when dealing with finances, withdrawing from hobbies, and experiencing increased anxiety or confusion.
